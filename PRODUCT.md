# Athenaeum — Product Specification

## Product Mechanism
A quiet sanctuary library management and circulation system built for scholars, students, and readers with real-time multi-branch availability, smart reservation holds, and curated digital archives.

## Target Audience
Scholars, university students, researchers, bibliophiles, and library admins who value quiet focus, elegant organization, and frictionless borrowing.

## Core Capabilities
- Multi-branch real-time catalog search & physical copy availability.
- Smart reservations, active loans management & automated renewal alerts.
- Digital sanctuary archives & rare manuscripts collection.
- Member circulation management & administrative analytics.

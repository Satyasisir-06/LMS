# Athenaeum — Design System & Aesthetic Language

## Mode
Persuade (Landing Page) & Operate (Dashboard & Admin).

## Aesthetic World
Quiet Luxury Sanctuary: Deep warm obsidian (`#0c0a09`), aged parchment tones (`#f5efe6`), glowing amber/gold accents (`#f59e0b` / `#d97706`), serif typography (`Playfair Display` / `Cinzel` / `serif`), and refined micro-interactions.

## Color Strategy
- Background: Deep Stone / Warm Obsidian (`#0c0a09`, `#1c1917`).
- Card Surfaces: Glassmorphic Stone (`rgba(28,25,23,0.7)`) with warm amber border highlights (`rgba(245,158,11,0.15)`).
- Primary Accent: Rich Amber / Warm Gold (`#f59e0b`, `#d97706`).
- Text Hierarchy: Amber Gold Titles (`#fef3c7`), Stone/Parchment Body (`#e7e5e4`), Muted Slate Accents (`#a8a29e`).

## Layout & Motion
- Immersive Hero section with floating 3D-styled interactive book showcases and live search.
- Responsive grid structure with subtle border glows and micro-animations.
- Rich typography hierarchy with serif headings and clean sans-serif body.
